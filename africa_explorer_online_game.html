🏅 Mission report

Explorer's reference desk

Use this desk after playing to revise. Africa is bordered by the Mediterranean Sea to the north, the Atlantic Ocean to the west, the Indian Ocean to the east and south-east, and the Southern Ocean is sometimes shown south of Africa on world maps. The Equator crosses central Africa. Madagascar is an island country off the south-eastern coast. Zanzibar is an island group belonging to Tanzania.

All 54 African countries and commonly listed capitals

Capital arrangements can be complex. South Africa has three capitals; Eswatini has administrative and royal/legislative capitals; Benin's official capital is Porto-Novo; Côte d'Ivoire's official capital is Yamoussoukro. Equatorial Guinea designated Ciudad de la Paz as its capital in 2026, although the transfer of government functions is gradual.
